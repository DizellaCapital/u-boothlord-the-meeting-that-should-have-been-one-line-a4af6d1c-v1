One sentence that could have replaced an entire pointless meeting — a [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty entry by [@dizellacapital](https://make.fun/r/qxacafna).

## The line

> **The budget was approved on Tuesday, the client said no on Wednesday, and this meeting is Thursday pretending neither of those things happened.**

The budget was approved on Tuesday and the client said no on Wednesday — so Thursday's meeting is just everyone pretending neither of those things happened.

## How it works

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes the line to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (fallback `/output`), validates it is a single line of 1–200 characters with no tabs and sentence-ending punctuation, and verifies the write by reading it back. The entry file is exactly 142 bytes, with no trailing newline.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat "$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt"
```
